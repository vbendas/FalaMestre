## Overview

This document outlines the financial model for Fala Mestre! as a **Socios.com user acquisition channel**. While the project can operate as a standalone business, its primary value proposition is as a **regulatory-compliant, ultra-low-cost acquisition channel** for Socios.com fan tokens.

> [!important] Partnership Value Proposition
> **Socios.com achieves 10-50x lower CAC ($0.80 vs $50-200) while accessing 70M+ Brazilian football fans through a regulatory-compliant channel.**
