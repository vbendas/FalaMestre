## Overview

Fala Mestre! launches exclusively in Brazil, leveraging the country's passion for football, high mobile adoption, and growing interest in fan tokens. The strategy prioritizes viral growth over paid acquisition.

> [!important] Launch Philosophy
> "Build something people want to share, then give them reasons to share it."

## Launch Timeline

### Phase 1: Stealth (Weeks 1-2)

```yaml
Goals:
  - Build hype without revealing product
  - Secure influencer commitments
  - Test core mechanics with select users

Activities:
  - Private beta (100 users max)
  - Influencer NDA briefings
  - Teaser social content
  - Domain/social handle setup
  
Metrics:
  - Beta feedback score: >8/10
  - Influencer commitments: 10+
  - Social followers: 1,000+
```

### Phase 2: Soft Launch (Weeks 3-4)

```yaml
Goals:
  - Validate product-market fit
  - Identify and fix issues
  - Build initial user base

Activities:
  - Open beta (invite-only)
  - Limited marketing
  - Community Discord launch
  - First tournament
  
Metrics:
  - Users: 1,000-3,000
  - D7 retention: >35%
  - NPS: >30
  - Critical bugs: 0
```

### Phase 3: Public Launch (Weeks 5-8)

```yaml
Goals:
  - Maximize awareness
  - Drive user acquisition
  - Establish market position

Activities:
  - Full public access
  - Influencer campaign launch
  - PR push (tech + sports media)
  - Paid ads begin
  - Club partnerships announced
  
Metrics:
  - Users: 10,000-25,000
  - CAC: <$2
  - D7 retention: >40%
  - Viral coefficient: >0.8
```

### Phase 4: Scale (Weeks 9-12)

```yaml
Goals:
  - Accelerate growth
  - Optimize monetization
  - Build competitive moat

Activities:
  - Major tournament (prize pool)
  - TV/media appearances
  - Influencer expansion
  - Feature updates
  
Metrics:
  - Users: 50,000-100,000
  - CAC: <$1
  - Revenue: Break-even
  - Viral coefficient: >1.2
```

## Paid Advertising

### Budget Allocation (First 6 Months)

| Month | Budget | Focus |
|-------|--------|-------|
| 1 | $1,000 | Testing creatives |
| 2 | $3,000 | Scaling winners |
| 3 | $5,000 | Launch push |
| 4 | $8,000 | Growth acceleration |
| 5 | $10,000 | Optimization |
| 6 | $12,000 | Scale |

### Platform Strategy

**Facebook/Instagram (50% of paid)**
- Format: Stories, Reels, Feed
- Targeting: Football interests, Mobile gamers
- Creative: Match screenshots, AI conversations
- CPA Target: $0.50

**TikTok (25% of paid)**
- Format: In-feed video
- Targeting: Football, Gaming, 18-35
- Creative: Funny AI moments, dramatic matches
- CPA Target: $0.40

**Google (20% of paid)**
- Search: "football manager mobile", "jogo de futebol"
- YouTube: Pre-roll on football content
- CPA Target: $0.80

**Twitter (5% of paid)**
- Targeting: Football fans, Team followers
- Creative: Real-time match reactions
- CPA Target: $1.00

### Creative Strategy

**Winning Ad Formats:**

1. **"AI Reacts" Format**
   - Show funny/dramatic AI commentary
   - Hook: "When the AI describes your goal..."
   
2. **"Match Result" Format**
   - Show exciting match result screenshot
   - Hook: "Meu time acabou de ganhar do Real Madrid por 4-0"
   
3. **"Earn Real Tokens" Format**
   - Show point accumulation
   - Hook: "Playing this game earned me real fan tokens"
   
4. **"ChatGPT for Football" Format**
   - Show conversation interface
   - Hook: "Imagine ChatGPT was your football manager..."

## Partnership Strategy

### Club Partnerships

**Phase 1 (Launch):**
- Unofficial support (no licensing required)
- Use public team data
- Community-driven team updates

**Phase 2 (Post-traction):**
- Approach 2-3 clubs with data
- Offer user acquisition value
- Negotiate licensing deals

**Target Clubs (via Socios.com partnership):**
Clubs with largest fanbases and Socios.com fan tokens will be prioritized for official licensing post-partnership. Fictional teams used in MVP demonstrate the model without IP risk.

### Socios.com Partnership

| Phase | Relationship |
|-------|--------------|
| MVP | Independent development |
| Soft Launch | API integration |
| Public Launch | Co-marketing |
| Scale | Official partnership |
| Exit | Potential acquisition |
